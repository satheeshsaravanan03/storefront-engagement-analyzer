# Exhibition Monitoring: what the app does and what video we need

A short brief for anyone helping us find demo videos. No technical background needed.

## What the app does

Imagine a product stand at an exhibition, or a display in a shop, with a camera watching the visitors. We upload that camera's video and the app tells us:

1. **Who stopped and looked at the display**, and for how long. People who only walk past are ignored.
2. **How interested each person was**: glance, looked, engaged or strongly engaged, based on how many seconds they faced the display.
3. **Who they are, if they registered.** At an exhibition, visitors can sign up beforehand with a photo, name, email and phone. The app compares each interested viewer's face with those photos and shows the name and contact details on a card. If nobody matches, it shows "Unmatched".

So the sales team can see, after the event, "these 12 people were really interested, and here is how to contact the ones who registered".

Face matching only applies to people who registered and agreed to it.

## The video we need

The demo video should look like this:

- **A fixed camera**, not handheld, with no cuts, zooms or edits.
- **Placed at the display, at about eye level**, pointing at the visitors, so that when people look at the product they also look toward the camera.
- **Clear, large faces.** A face should be at least about 60 pixels wide, which means people stand within a few metres of the camera.
- **Good light on the face.** No strong light behind the people.
- **People actually stopping and looking**, for 5 seconds or more. We want a mix: some walk past, some glance, some stand and look for a while.
- **Quality:** 1080p or better, about 2 to 5 minutes, MP4 if possible.
- **Few people at once in the foreground**, 1 to 6, so nobody is hidden behind someone else.

## What does NOT work well

- Ceiling or high-corner CCTV looking down. Faces are tilted away and too small.
- Wide shots of a whole hall or street. Faces end up only 20 to 40 pixels wide.
- Movie reviews, interviews and vlogs. People talk to the camera, so there is no "looking at a display" behaviour.
- Masks, sunglasses, caps pulled low, or people mostly turned away.
- Blurry, dark or heavily compressed footage.
- Videos with cuts between different cameras or scenes.

With poor footage the app still counts who stopped, but it cannot name them. It shows "No face" or "Unmatched" instead.

## Where to search

Try these search terms on Pexels, Pixabay, Mixkit, Videvo and YouTube:

- exhibition booth visitors
- trade show stand visitors
- expo stall customers looking at product
- product display customers looking
- retail store customers browsing shelf
- shop window shoppers looking
- supermarket customers looking at products
- mall kiosk people stopping
- product launch demo audience (only if it is a stand-style setup)

When you find a candidate, check against the list above, then send the link and a note on which points it meets.

## Important notes

- **Licence.** Only use footage we are allowed to use in a client demo. Stock sites like Pexels and Pixabay usually allow it, but check each video. Do not download from YouTube without checking the licence.
- **Stock footage will not have registered faces.** Everyone in it will show as "Unmatched", which is fine for showing the detection and attention part.
- **For the full demo with real name cards, we need our own recording.** A few of us act as visitors at a mock stand with a phone or webcam on a tripod. Each person registers with a normal photo first. This is the best way to show the client the complete flow.
- **Privacy.** Never use footage of identifiable real people without permission for anything beyond the demo, and do not share people's photos or contact details.

## Quick checklist for each video

- [ ] Camera is fixed and at about eye level
- [ ] Faces look clearly visible and not tiny
- [ ] Good light on faces
- [ ] At least a few people stop and look at something for 5 seconds or more
- [ ] 1080p or better, 2 to 5 minutes
- [ ] No cuts between scenes
- [ ] We are allowed to use it
